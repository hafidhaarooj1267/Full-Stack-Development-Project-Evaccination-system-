How ImmunoTrack Works

Three simple steps to protect your child
1

Register & Add Children

Create a parent account and add your child’s details. The system automatically generates a recommended vaccination schedule.

2

Book Appointment

Choose a hospital, select a vaccine and confirm your preferred date & time. Receive instant confirmation.

3

Get Vaccinated & Track

Visit the hospital on scheduled date. Hospital updates the status. You get digital certificate and reminders for next dose.
