Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register my child?
Simply create a parent account, then go to "My Children" and add details. You'll see recommended vaccines.
Can hospitals update vaccine availability?
Yes, hospitals have a dedicated dashboard to manage stock and mark vaccinations as complete.
Is the data secure?
Absolutely. All data is encrypted and stored locally (demo version) or on secure servers.
